## AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) Rules
1. **Lead with the answer**: A short direct-answer block (roughly 40 to 60 words) at the top of a page or under each heading. Don't bury the point (AI Overview citations heavily favor the first third of a page).
2. **Topic Coverage > Rank**: Ranking on exact queries matters less than covering the surrounding questions comprehensively. Answer follow-up questions to get pulled into related AI queries.
3. **Freshness**: Maintain "last updated" dates and refresh top pages on a schedule.
4. **Schema**: Schema is useful but *must* accurately reflect the visible content.
