What is QuickBooks cleanup and catch-up bookkeeping?

QuickBooks cleanup involves fixing existing errors in your accounting software, such as duplicate entries, unreconciled bank accounts, and misclassified expenses. Catch-up bookkeeping is the process of entering months or years of missing financial data to bring your books current so you can file accurate taxes or secure a loan.

The cost of ignoring messy books.

Bad bookkeeping doesn't just annoy your CPA. It costs you money through overpaid taxes, missed deductions, and denied loans.
