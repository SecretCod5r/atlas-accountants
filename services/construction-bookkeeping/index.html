What does construction bookkeeping include?

Construction bookkeeping tracks direct job costs—labor, materials, and subcontractors—against specific projects rather than lumping them into general overhead. It includes managing draw schedules, tracking retainage, reconciling progress billing, and producing Work in Progress (WIP) reports so you know your exact gross margin on every job.
