What is bookkeeping for real estate investors?

Bookkeeping for real estate investors involves tracking income and expenses strictly on a per-property or per-door basis. It requires cleanly separating routine repairs from capital improvements (CapEx) to ensure accurate depreciation schedules and to provide clean P&L statements for lenders when refinancing.
